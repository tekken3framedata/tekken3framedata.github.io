1,2,2,3,2,4,3 Devil Jin Combo 3,4,5,13,4,4,21 hhhhhmh #7
Foot Notes #1 Execute the command during the Lightning Force animation. The Possession will be activated after the Lightning Force.
#2 A fast command input will cancel the Lightning Force and the Devil Jin Possession is immediately activated.
#3 This is similar to Heihachi's Thunder Godfist, you can not do the Mid Kick or Heel Sweep extension.
#4 Causes an incredible 27 frames of Guard Break when blocked. And it hits MID! Kazuya and Jun take 13 of Guard Damage.
#5 Is always a clean hit and will never cause Trip Stun.
#6 This throw is not escapable. During this throw you can see the markings on Jin's forehead and the red glowing eyes.
#7 This string is a true combo, once the left jab hits the rest cannot be blocked.

Devil Jin can NOT be selected as a character. But Jin can be TEMPORARILY possessed by Devil Jin ONLY when Heihachi is teamed with Jin. It will end after doing one move, walking back, side stepping, blocking an attack, jumping, ducking or tagging out. Walking forward, dashing forward and dashing back is possible while keeping Devil Jin Possession.
Most of the Devil Jin moves have the same commands as regular Jin moves, so you will not be able to do these moves while in Devil Jin Possession. You will notice that Jin takes on Heihachi's stance while in Devil Jin Possession and lightning will travel around his body constantly.
